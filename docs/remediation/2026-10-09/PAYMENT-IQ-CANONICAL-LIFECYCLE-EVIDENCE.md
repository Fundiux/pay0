## Observed executable contracts

Claim (iqExecution.ts:590) rereads canonical plan/root/hash/execution status, reservation, payment, plan item, lock, application, solicitud and company inside the transaction. It refuses changed application amount, reservation linkage, root/company/lock scope, unknown or concurrent execution. SUCCEEDED goes through executionResultFromPlan, which requires matching IQ_APPLIED/SUCCEEDED and returns the stored result. A succeeded pending-folio plan reuses that result rather than admitting another claim. This test stops at the claim and therefore does not claim to have exercised HTTP POST suppression directly.

Finalize (iqExecution.ts:902) rereads plan + attempt and requires matching current attempt. It rereads each lock and rejects replaced/missing lock fences. It merges the CURRENT nested canonical plan, not an extra caller-supplied plan snapshot. Accepted success without numeric folio becomes CREATED_PENDING_FOLIO; numeric folio becomes CREATED_CONFIRMED. NOT_SUBMITTED plus submitClicked=false becomes FAILED_SAFE/PREVALIDATED; submitStarted/UNKNOWN/REJECTED become review states. Tests check plan/lock projections, financial amounts unchanged, retry fence rejection and rollback of all staged projections.

These tests support a future scoped replacement for the obsolete browser marker. They do not prove that every captured item/identity field is refreshed at finalize: the function still uses claimed.items and the supplied result after checking attempt fences. No blanket immutable-snapshot safety certification.
