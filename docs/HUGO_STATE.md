### Learned rules and observations audit

`agent007LearnedRules` records are created only when a superadmin resolves a pending recommendation in `resolveAgent007Recommendation`. A transaction increments approvals or rejections on a key derived from root, kind and proposal; it stores last decision and optional correction. `loadConversationState` retrieves the 20 newest rules, and `contextBuilder` passes at most ten with `approvals > rejections` into `reglasConfirmadas` in the model context. These counters do not change tool authorization, financial state, or deterministic context selection. They may nevertheless be phrased as confirmed rules in the prompt after a single approval, which can promote a local decision into apparent truth without independent verification. Preserve the decision, source recommendation, actor, correction, counts and validity history for Memory v2; separate an approval from an independently verified fact.

`agent007Observations` is written by the explicit superadmin observation callable and by `observer.ts` for its `OBSERVED_EVENTS` activity allowlist. Activity observations store root/case identity, source event/activity, intent, actor, `humanDecision` (currently actor role, not an actual decision), outcome (activity description), signal severity/module/category, amount and selected references, timestamps and authorization metadata. Explicit observations require case, intent, humanDecision and outcome but lack structured severity and source activity. `/hugo` lists observations. `loadConversationState` does not retrieve them, and `contextBuilder` does not feed them to Gemini or deterministic tools. Their current effect on future replies and decisions is therefore zero through the conversation path; they are primarily an audit/UI store. Memory v2 should distinguish observation, human decision and verified outcome before promoting any of them to reusable memory.

### Evaluation taxonomy and next phase

The failure taxonomy is `RETRIEVAL_FAILURE`, `CONTEXT_FAILURE`, `MODEL_REASONING_FAILURE`, `HALLUCINATION`, `PROVENANCE_FAILURE`, `AUTHORIZATION_FAILURE`, `TOOL_FAILURE`, `POLICY_FAILURE`, `MEMORY_FAILURE`, `CONVERSATION_REFERENCE_FAILURE`, `FALLBACK_FAILURE`. Assign multiple labels only with evidence. Current observed labels are model reasoning/hallucination for the sample total and conversation reference for the deterministic follow-up; no authorization or tool failure was observed in Phase 3 emulator checks. The five selected golden cases and acceptance criteria are in `phase3-golden-cases.json`. Phase 4 should make completeness explicit in context, resolve unique conversational references while asking for clarification on ambiguity, and then test whether typed memory solves any remaining failures that actually require past experience.
